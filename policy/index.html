Privacy & Cookie Policy

Togari Onsen Ski Resort Co., Ltd. (“the Company”) sets out the following Privacy Policy (“this Policy”) on how it handles users' personal information in connection with the services provided on this website (“the Service”).

1. Management of personal information

The Company keeps customers' personal information accurate and up to date. To prevent unauthorised access, loss, damage, alteration or leakage, it maintains security systems and management frameworks and trains its employees thoroughly. Personal information is managed strictly with appropriate safety measures.

2. Purpose of use

Personal information entrusted to the Company is used to contact customers, provide information about services and respond to inquiries, including by email and by sending materials.

3. Disclosure to third parties

The Company manages customers' personal information properly and will not disclose it to third parties except:

  • when the customer consents to the disclosure;
  • when disclosure to contractors entrusted by the Company is necessary to provide the services the customer requested;
  • when disclosure is required by law.

4. Security measures

The Company applies thorough security measures to keep personal information accurate and safe.

5. Requests from individuals

If you wish to inquire about, correct or delete your personal information, the Company will respond after verifying your identity.

6. Cookies

This website does not use analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, and it does not load third-party tracking scripts. If that changes, non-essential cookies will only be set after you give consent, and this section will be updated.

7. Compliance and review

The Company complies with applicable Japanese laws and other regulations on the personal information it holds, and will review and improve this Policy as necessary.
